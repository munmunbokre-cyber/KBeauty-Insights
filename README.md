## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer reviews of Korean beauty products to understand customer opinions, product ratings, satisfaction levels, and sentiment patterns.

The analysis explores customer feedback to identify product performance trends, common concerns, and useful business insights.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze customer ratings of Korean beauty products.
- Understand customer preferences across skin types.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction.
- Compare product performance.
- Analyze positive and negative customer sentiments.
- Identify common keywords in Korean customer reviews.
- Extract meaningful insights from customer feedback.
